# Product Epics

| Epic | Stories | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **E1 — Data Collection & Project Setup** | US-01–US-05 | Connect GitHub, retrieve commits/PRs/issues/sprint data, and configure projects and contributor mappings. |
| **E2 — Health & Risk Analysis** | US-06–US-10 | Detect bottlenecks, possible workload imbalance, stale issues, and indicative schedule risk using configurable thresholds. |
| **E3 — Explanations, Reports & Recommendations** | US-11–US-15 | Explain flags, generate reports and advisory recommendations, schedule delivery to dashboard/email/Slack, and export PDF. |
| **E4 — Dashboard & User Feedback** | US-16–US-17 | Present unified project health and provide a feedback/appeal workflow. |
| **E5 — Access Control & Administration** | US-18–US-21 | Provide RBAC, integration configuration, authentication, and project/user lifecycle administration. Audit logging is covered by NFR-10 across epics, not a separate user story. |
